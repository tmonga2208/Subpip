Shipping and Delivery Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

A digital product: nothing is shipped

SubPIP Premium is a license for the SubPIP browser extension. It is delivered online, so nothing is shipped to you and there are no shipping charges.

How and when you get it

As soon as your payment goes through, your license key is shown on the payment page and sent by email to the address you entered at checkout, usually within a few minutes. If you started the purchase from the extension while signed in, Premium is also switched on for that account by itself.

If the email does not arrive

Check your spam folder first. You can have the key sent again with "Lost your license key?" on the Premium page. If you still have nothing an hour after paying, email tarunmonga2208@gmail.com from the address you bought with, or include your Razorpay payment ID, and we will send it ourselves.

Where it is available

Delivery is by email, so it reaches you in any country. Premium works wherever you use SubPIP signed in to the account the license is activated on.

Refunds

You can get a full refund within 7 days of purchase. See the Refund Policy.
