SubPIP is installed

Three steps to your first floating video with its subtitles.

  1. Pin the icon

    Click the puzzle piece in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to SubPIP, so it is one click away.

  2. Play a video

    On YouTube, Netflix or any other site, with its captions turned on.

  3. Press Alt + P

    Or click the SubPIP icon and choose Open Picture-in-Picture. On a Mac the Alt key is called Option.

If something is off

  • No captions in the window? Click the SubPIP icon and choose "Captions not showing? Pick them on the page", then click a subtitle once. SubPIP remembers it for that site.
  • A site does not work? Click the SubPIP icon and choose "Not working on this site? Tell me". It takes ten seconds and it is how sites get fixed.
  • Want another key? Open chrome://extensions/shortcuts and set your own.
  • Anonymous usage counts are on from version 4.8: how often the window is opened and whether captions were found, with no name and no page address. They show where SubPIP fails. Switch them off under "Usage counts" in the popup. What is sent
