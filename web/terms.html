1. The service

SubPIP is a browser extension that plays videos in Picture-in-Picture with subtitles. The free version is provided as is. SubPIP Premium adds caption translation, playback speed control and subtitles from a VTT/SRT link.

2. Premium license

Premium is a one-time payment for a lifetime license, for as long as SubPIP is offered, or a smaller one-time payment for one year. The price depends on where you are and is shown at checkout before you pay. A one-year license ends a year after purchase; it is not renewed by itself and nothing is charged again unless you buy again. We email a reminder about a week before it ends. A license activates on one SubPIP account and works in every browser where you sign in to that account. Licenses are personal: please don't share your account or key.

3. Payments and refunds

Payments are processed by Razorpay. You can get a full refund within 7 days of purchase, no questions asked; see the Refund Policy. A refunded license is deactivated.
