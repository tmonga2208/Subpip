Watch a video in a floating window and keep its subtitles.

Chrome's own Picture-in-Picture drops the captions. SubPIP opens the video in a floating window that keeps them, with real controls: play, seek, volume and a captions switch. Keep working in another tab and still follow every line.

HOW TO USE
1. Play a video with its captions turned on.
2. Press Alt+P, or click the SubPIP icon and choose Open Picture-in-Picture.
3. The video opens in a floating window with its subtitles.

WHERE IT WORKS
• YouTube, Netflix, JioHotstar and Crunchyroll, with caption support built in.
• Most other sites, using the video's own captions. If SubPIP cannot find a site's captions, point at them once and it remembers.
• Chrome, Edge and Brave on a computer.

FREE

PRIVACY
The extension has no ads and does not collect your browsing history. It sends anonymous usage counts (how often the window is opened and whether captions were found, with no name and no page address), which you can switch off in the popup. Your settings stay in your browser. Caption text leaves your browser only when you turn translation on, and not at all where Chrome can translate on your device.
